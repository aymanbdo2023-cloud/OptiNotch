Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

OptiNotch is a Windows desktop application that displays a small overlay bar at the top of your screen. This policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and what we never do with it.

In short: OptiNotch stores everything locally on your own computer. It does not use analytics, does not show ads, and does not send your data to any server we operate.

Google Calendar data

Calendar features are optional and require you to sign in with your own Google account. OptiNotch uses Google's official OAuth 2.0 flow and requests read-only access to your Google Calendar events (calendar.readonly). It can view but cannot create, modify, or delete events.

Calendar data is used solely to display your events inside the app. It is:

  • Fetched directly from Google only after you sign in.
  • Kept only in the app's memory while running.
  • Never uploaded to our servers, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

Now-playing media information

When enabled, OptiNotch uses the Windows Media (SMTC) service to show the title, artist, and album art of whatever is currently playing on your system. This information stays on your computer and is only used to render the media display.

Settings

Your preferences — monitor selection, position, accent color, opacity, and whether features are enabled — are stored in a local configuration file in %APPDATA%\OptiNotch\settings.json. Nothing leaves your machine.

Sign-in credentials and tokens

When you connect your Google account, an access token and refresh token are stored locally in %APPDATA%\OptiNotch\gcal_token.json. These tokens are used only to request your calendar from Google and are transmitted only to Google as part of the standard OAuth flow. Signing out of the app deletes this token file. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information on our servers.
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or tracking of any kind.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

Third-party services

The only third-party service the app communicates with is Google, and only when you choose to connect your Google Calendar. Google's handling of your data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

Because the app does not store your data on any server, there is nothing for us to delete on request. To remove locally stored data, uninstall the app and delete the %APPDATA%\OptiNotch folder. To revoke Google access, use the Sign out option in the app or your Google account settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes take effect when the "Last updated" date at the top of this page changes.
